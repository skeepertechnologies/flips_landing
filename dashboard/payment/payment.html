How to Pay via M-Pesa Paybill
  • Go to Safaricom Menu > M-Pesa > Lipa na M-Pesa > Paybill.
  • Enter the Paybill Number below.
  • Enter the Account Number below (or your phone number).
  • Enter the Amount and confirm the payment.
  • Enter the M-Pesa Transaction ID received via SMS below.
How to Pay via M-Pesa STK Push
  • Enter your phone number below.
  • Click 'Initiate STK Push' to receive a payment prompt on your phone.
  • Enter your M-Pesa PIN to authorize the payment.
  • Wait for confirmation and verify the payment using the Transaction ID.
